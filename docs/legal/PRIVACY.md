# Chettik Privacy Policy

**Status:** Stage 1 placeholder  
**Language:** English (Russian version to be legally reviewed alongside this text)

## 1. Data used to operate the service

Chettik processes a phone number, profile information, and session/device information to provide an account. Cloud-chat content is processed to deliver messages. Secret chats are planned as device-bound end-to-end encrypted conversations and require separate architecture and security review.

## 2. Your privacy controls

The planned privacy model supports audiences of everybody, nobody, contacts, and exceptions for selected profile and messaging fields. Users can manage profile information, blocks, sessions, and account deletion.

## 3. Retention and rights

The production policy will disclose retention periods, account and message deletion behavior, data access/deletion requests, security contacts, and rights applicable in each launch market.

## 4. Status of this document

This is an implementation placeholder, not final legal advice or a published production policy. Counsel must review and replace it before public launch.
